Born. Grown. Dissolved. Born again.

A small breathing organism made of sound. Life and death, held in one slow pulse. Healing in the overtones, peace in the dust.

Nothing is composed. It lives, and it passes.

Sometimes you have to stay away and just let the THING go. Push the sliders all the way left, or all the way right. Switch the buttons off. Switch them on. Do nothing. Let it play itself.

Manual
Press for planting seed(s)
Press and slide left right (frequency adjust)
Sliders for life & death times
Press 3 or 4 times under 1 second for Arpeggio Sound / flowers
2 fingers for kick on or off
3 fingers for double bpm kick

Listen on headphones or your sound system for the best experience.

Let it go, let it grow, let it die, let it come to you!
